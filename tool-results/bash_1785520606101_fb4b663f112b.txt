Instead of 29 flat tabs, users see a curated sidebar navigation tailored to their role. Citizens see Overview, Report Event, Map, Community Feed, My Evidence, Missions, Rewards, AI Copilot. Government Officials see National Dashboard, Analytics, Investigations, Inspections, Cases, Simulation, Fraud Detection, Auto-Investigator, AI Copilot. Platform Admins see all of that plus Security, Performance, Developer, Production, Civil Trust, Settings. The collapsible sidebar, command palette (Cmd+K), keyboard shortcuts, role switcher, notifications, dark mode, and mobile-responsive drawer make the platform feel like a modern SaaS product. The personalized welcome card with stat cards and activity feed creates a delightful first impression for each user type.
