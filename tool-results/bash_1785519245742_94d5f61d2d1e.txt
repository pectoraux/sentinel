Stage Summary:
- Autonomous Investigation Engine is COMPLETE and browser-verified.
- This is the "wow factor" feature that transforms Sentinel from a reporting platform into an AI-powered environmental investigation system. When an Intelligence Event is created — by a citizen or by the Digital Twin — the AI automatically becomes an investigator. It runs a 7-phase autonomous workflow: (1) gathers nearby historical events for spatial/temporal context, (2) compares recent satellite imagery with older imagery for change detection, (3) identifies affected rivers, forests, and communities within the impact zone, (4) auto-creates evidence requests (missions) for trusted nearby contributors, (5) generates a full reasoning chain explaining why the event is credible or not, (6) continuously updates confidence using Bayesian updates as new evidence arrives, and (7) recommends the next best action (dispatch inspector, request drone, escalate, wait for corroboration, dismiss, monitor, request lab analysis, or notify agency). The confidence updates use standard Bayesian inference: posterior = (prior × likelihood) / (prior × likelihood + (1-prior)), with each piece of evidence adjusting the likelihood ratio. The AI generates a human-readable credibility assessment explaining its reasoning.
